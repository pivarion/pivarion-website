Credits

2013 Ferrari LaFerrari

3D model by XENVOR creations, available on Sketchfab under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.

Adaptations: levelled export orientation, scale, black paint, surface finishes, lighting, wheel rigging, stationary calipers, and brake effects. The downloaded GLB is retained unchanged. The framed car image is rendered from this model.

Pivarion studio furniture and equipment

The textured Camera 01, Sofa 02, and Classic Laptop, Metal Office Desk, and Modern Arm Chair 01 models are by Poly Haven contributors and are available under CC0 1.0.

The models are measured to real studio dimensions, finished in the Pivarion palette, and combined with modeled tripods and architecture.

Pivarion artwork and reference photography

Logo and vehicle reference photos supplied by the project owner. Flat logo placements use the original JPG. The 3D logo is an extrusion of contours sampled from that JPG; the supplied six-view study guides its depth and finish.

Three.js

Three.js r150 and its glTF loader are used under the MIT license. The loader is adapted from the official r150 source to share the existing Three.js runtime.
