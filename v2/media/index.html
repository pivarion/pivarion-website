OUR GOAL

The biggest car hub in North America. Not a page that reposts — a place cars are actually filmed, owners are actually known, and a shop can put a build in front of a real audience the week it is finished. We are building it outward from where we are.

  1. I

    Toronto and the GTA

    The home ground. Weekly shoots, meets, and the shops doing the work that deserves a camera on it.

  2. II

    Ontario, then the corridor

    Hamilton to Ottawa, then down through the border cities — the same format, run by people who live there.

  3. III

    Coast to coast

    One channel, every scene worth filming, and a directory that a buyer in another province can actually use.
